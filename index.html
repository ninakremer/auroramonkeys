About us

The story is short: we grew up in the very North in the 90s. We know the roads, we watch the forecasts, we look at the sky, and we live by one important rule: if it’s meant to be then it will be.

Aurora Monkeys is a small local company creating experiences in groups of up to 8–16 guests. No giant buses, just comfortable Japanese vans. We don’t talk too much either. We’d rather listen to Sade while driving, enjoy the views and let the North do the talking.
